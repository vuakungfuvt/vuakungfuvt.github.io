Kurrent Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026
Developer / data controller: Phan Thanh Tung, Vietnam
Contact: phanthanhtung.pt@gmail.com

Kurrent ("the app", "we") is a currency converter for Android. This policy explains what information the app and the third-party services it uses handle. We wrote it to match what the app actually does.

1. Summary

  • Kurrent has no accounts. You do not sign up, log in or give us your name, email or phone number.
  • We do not collect personal data ourselves and we do not run our own servers that receive your data. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob (Google) collects some device and usage information to serve and measure ads (section 3).
  • Your amounts, favorites, settings and downloaded rates are stored only on your device (section 5).
  • The app downloads exchange rates from public third-party services, which can see your IP address (section 4).

We do not read your contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or precise location. The app does not request a location permission.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob) and consent

Kurrent is free and supported by ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. When the app requests or shows an ad, the AdMob SDK may automatically collect and send to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) and, where available, an app-set ID;
  • your IP address, which Google can use to estimate your approximate location (the app itself never reads your location);
  • device and software information (for example device model, OS version, language, screen size, network type);
  • information about how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information such as launch time and performance data.

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, to prevent fraud, and for analytics. It may personalize ads where you have allowed it. Google's practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Kurrent does not receive your advertising ID and does not combine it with any other information.

Your choices

  • EEA, UK and other regions where consent is required: on first launch the app shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can review or change your choice any time in the app under Settings > Privacy options (shown where required).
  • Android system settings: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in Settings > Google > Ads (the exact path varies by device), or delete your advertising ID there.
  • Remove all ads: the one-time "Remove Ads" purchase stops ad requests. After you purchase, the app does not initialize or request ads.
  • Kurrent does not tag its ads as child-directed because the app is not directed to children (section 8).

4. Exchange-rate sources (third-party requests)

To get rates the app connects directly from your device to public services. They receive the technical data any web request includes (your IP address, request time, user-agent) and may log it under their own policies. We send no account, advertising ID or other identifier to them.

  • fawazahmed0 Currency API, delivered through the jsDelivr CDN (cdn.jsdelivr.net) and a Cloudflare Pages mirror (currency-api.pages.dev) — https://github.com/fawazahmed0/exchange-api, https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy
  • Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev) — https://frankfurter.dev
  • ExchangeRate-API open access (open.er-api.com) — https://www.exchangerate-api.com/terms
  • Our configuration file (a small static file on https://vuakungfuvt.github.io) that may tell the app a minimum supported version and ad-frequency settings. It is hosted on GitHub Pages / a static host that can see your IP address. The request contains no user identifier.

The app tries these sources in turn and caches the result, so many launches make no request at all.

5. Local storage and retention

Settings, favorites and cached rates are kept on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. The purchase flag is kept in the device's secure storage and is not included in Android backups (backups are disabled for the app). We do not keep any copy.

6. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The "Remove Ads" in-app purchase is processed entirely by Google Play (Google Play Billing). Google handles your payment details and purchase records under its own terms and privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We never see your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether you own the product and stores the yes/no result on your device. Use "Restore purchases" in Settings after reinstalling or on a new device.

7. Sharing

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it, because we do not collect it. Information handled by Google (AdMob, Google Play) and by the rate services is described above. When you use the share or copy button, the content you choose goes to the app you pick through the Android share sheet; we do not control that app. Links you open (for example this policy) open in your browser.

8. Children

Kurrent is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the minimum digital-consent age in your country, up to 16). If you believe a child has used the app, contact us at phanthanhtung.pt@gmail.com and, because we hold no account data, we will help with any ad-ID deletion steps described above.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you reside (for example the EEA/UK under the GDPR, or certain US states such as California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because Kurrent holds no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete; the data described in section 3 is held by Google and requests about it go to Google (https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy). You can withdraw ad consent in the app (Settings > Privacy options) and delete local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling. For anything else, email phanthanhtung.pt@gmail.com.

10. Security

All network requests use HTTPS. The purchase flag is kept in the platform secure storage. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version; material changes will be reflected in the app update notes or on this page.
