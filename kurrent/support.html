FAQ

How often are rates updated? Rates are updated daily from public data sources. The data date is shown in the app. Rates are for reference only.

Why does my bank show a different rate? Banks, cards and exchange offices add margins and use their own rates. Kurrent shows reference rates. You can enter a custom rate for cash exchange.

Does it work offline? Yes. The last downloaded rates are saved on your device and the data date is shown.

How do I unlock the 1-year and 5-year charts? Watch one short optional ad to unlock them for 24 hours, or buy Remove Ads once to unlock them permanently.

How do I remove ads? Settings > Kurrent Pro card > Remove ads (one-time purchase through Google Play).

I bought Remove Ads but see ads / I reinstalled. Open Settings and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Google account on the device. If it still fails, email us with your Google Play order number.

How do I get a refund? Refunds for purchases are handled by Google Play: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637

How do I change my ad consent (EEA/UK)? Settings > Privacy options (shown where required).

How do I delete my data? Kurrent has no account and keeps data only on your device. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it. See the Privacy Policy for ad-ID controls.

A currency is missing or a rate looks wrong. Email us the currency code and what you expected; we depend on public data sources and will report issues where possible.

Which languages are supported? English, Vietnamese, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), French, German, Italian, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese (Simplified), Arabic, Hindi, Indonesian, Turkish.
